Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 13, 2026 • Last Updated: November 13, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the software platform Application Backup by TechLIMS (the "Platform"), developed and maintained by Vipul K Jain ("Author", "we", "us"). The Platform is an enterprise self-hosted automated backup and disaster recovery tool designed to transfer scheduled archives to user-authorized cloud storage providers, including Google Drive.

2. Information Collection and Storage

The Platform is self-hosted and operates under a zero-knowledge architecture:

  • Zero Remote Telemetry: The Platform does not transmit analytics, logs, user tracking, or backup contents to any central third-party servers. All data remains strictly within your self-hosted infrastructure.
  • Credentials at Rest: Database passwords, SSH private keys, and cloud OAuth refresh tokens are stored locally on your server, encrypted using authenticated AES-256-GCM cryptography.

3. Google User Data & Limited Scopes (OAuth 2.0)

When you connect your Google Account to enable backup synchronization to Google Drive, the Platform requests only the non-sensitive scope:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file

Limited Use Policy: The Platform’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • The Platform only creates, views, updates, and deletes files and folders that were directly created by the Platform.
  • The Platform never accesses, scans, reads, or indexes any existing personal documents, photos, spreadsheets, or third-party files in your Google Drive.
  • Google user credentials and access tokens are never transferred, sold, or shared with third parties or advertising networks.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Backup files transferred to Google Drive or local storage are retained according to the user-configured sliding-window retention policy (default: 10 days). You may delete all stored credentials, tokens, and backups at any time directly through the Platform console or through your Google Account permissions page at Google Security Settings.
